A note on sources & methodology

This index was originally compiled August 9–10, 2026, and reflects publicly available reporting as of that date. It has since been expanded across several later research sessions, with additional entries located via web research and organized into the same format — original one- or two-sentence summaries, never excerpted text, with primary, opinion, and advocacy sources labeled distinctly from independent journalism. As with any research aid, verify anything you plan to rely on against the linked source directly, particularly for entries involving ongoing litigation or unresolved investigations.

All source records, dates, tags, and summaries live in a companion file, spokane_source_data.xml, which this page loads at runtime. That file is designed to be readable and editable on its own — open it in any text editor to browse, search, or revise the underlying data directly, independent of this page's presentation.

It is organized by topic rather than strictly by outlet, so coverage of a single event can be read across multiple local, state, and national sources in one place. A small number of entries are primary or advocacy sources rather than independent journalism — government press releases, a litigant's own case page, organizational About pages, and outlets sympathetic to the figures covered here — and are labeled accordingly rather than presented as neutral reporting.
