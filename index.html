A note on sources & methodology

This index was originally compiled August 9–10, 2026, and reflects publicly available reporting as of that date. It has since been expanded across several later research sessions, with additional entries located via web research and organized into the same format — original one- or two-sentence summaries, never excerpted text, with primary, opinion, and advocacy sources labeled distinctly from independent journalism.

A link-quality pass replaced syndication mirrors (AOL, Yahoo, PressReader and similar reprint hosts) with the original publisher's URL wherever that original could be located, and removed entries resting on content farms, scraper sites, dead domains, defunct publications, or personal blogs. As with any research aid, verify anything you plan to rely on against the linked source directly, particularly for entries involving ongoing litigation or unresolved investigations.

All source records, dates, tags, and summaries live in a companion file, spokane_source_data.xml, which this page loads at runtime. That file is designed to be readable and editable on its own — open it in any text editor to browse, search, or revise the underlying data directly, independent of this page's presentation.
